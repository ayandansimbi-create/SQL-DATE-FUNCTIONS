SQL Date Functions Exercise

In this exercise, I worked with 21 different tables to solve 21 unique SQL problems, writing and executing queries in Databricks for each scenario.

The tasks focused on applying a variety of SQL date functions, including DAYNAME, MONTHNAME, DATE_FORMAT, CURRENT_DATE, DATEDIFF, DATE_ADD, and DATE_TRUNC. Several exercises also required the use of CASE statements to categorize and derive meaningful business outcomes from the data.

Each question was designed around a real-world business scenario, requiring the analysis and manipulation of date-related data to generate valuable business insights. Through these exercises, I strengthened my ability to work with date functions, perform date-based calculations, and apply SQL logic to support data-driven decision-making.
